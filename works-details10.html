Unveiled Depths

In the course about multisensory design, me and Simone Guglielmetti (https://www.behance.net/simonegugliel1) were given the open assignment to create an experience for the EU4Ocean Coalition for Ocean Literacy. With the ocean as context, our aim was to provide an immersive XR experience to inspire curiosity and empathy for ocean biomes. We envisioned this as prelude to a museum experience.

Engaging the senses of via different kinds of sight, sound and haptics were essential in the player immersion. If I’m not making innovative tools/products, I love creatively directing and creating such a project as a personal passion of mine. In Gravity Sketch VR we collaborated on the level design with the ability to test walking through it at scale, later I built the entire environment myself in Unreal Engine 5 using free assets.
