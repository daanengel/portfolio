DigiPHY

World-renowned automotive design consultancy Granstudio had asked me to come to them in Turin, Italy, to work as the main concept designer of their XR product: DigiPHY. This proved to be a perfect project for me as it engaged all the aspects I love in a role; out-of-the-box, visionary and strategic thinking and creating.

DigiPHY is a mixed reality platform that connects VR, physical supports and phygital (physical + digital) validation for faster automotive design decisions by allowing designers to touch reconfigurable physical mockups while viewing fully immersive, digital 3D environments.

I mostly contributed to the future vision to add collaboration, both synchronous and asynchronous. I worked as a lead designer, going between ideation and lo-fi prototypes and had weekly update sessions directly with the CEO/creative director (Lowie Vermeersch) and the chief of interaction design. I worked on the open-ended start where I explored my wildest ideas and main future vision, all the way to the more concrete formation of my design documents with short-term features that were presented directly to our software engineers.

Role
Lead XR designer, concept designer
